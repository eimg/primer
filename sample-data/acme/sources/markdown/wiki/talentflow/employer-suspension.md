# TalentFlow employer suspension

Suspension prevents an employer account from creating or publishing new job listings. It also blocks new employer-user invitations.

Existing public listings remain visible until they expire or Trust explicitly unpublishes them. This preserves candidate access to jobs that are still under review and avoids silently changing active campaigns.

Suspension does not delete applications, interviews, or audit events.
